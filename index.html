How it Works

Six Unique Steps with Purpose-built Agents at Every One

  1. Step 01

    Connect & Scan

    Read-only token. Every file and method fingerprinted. A readiness verdict before any commitment.

  2. Step 02

    Instrument

    Build-config changes only — no application logic rewritten. Delivered as pull requests your team approves.

  3. Step 03

    Execute

    Your existing suites run unchanged, on your infrastructure. Manual sessions count identically.

  4. Step 04

    Collect

    Only coverage counters leave the app — no payloads, no personal data, per-test attribution as a delta.

  5. Step 05

    Map & Analyse

    Coverage matched to fingerprints, so refactoring never breaks the map. Risk-ranked gaps, per build.

  6. Step 06

    Act

    A prioritized Test Run Spec — what to run, what’s uncovered, and why — ready for your test team.
